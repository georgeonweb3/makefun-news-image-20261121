# The World in One Day — 21 November 2026

make.fun competition entry: **news-image-20261121**.

One image summing up world news of **Saturday 21 November 2026**, drawn
programmatically (Python + Pillow, no photos, no scraped images) as a
newspaper front page: a masthead naming the day, a tall hero panel
leading with the third straight night of Houthi missiles aimed at
Riyadh — all three intercepted before dawn (night sky, crescent moon,
Riyadh skyline with the PIF Tower, three interceptor streaks meeting
the missiles in bright bursts) — six reported stories in two compact
rows, and an "Also today" strip.

## Stories

- **Lead — Riyadh (today, pre-dawn 21 Nov):** Houthi rebels fire three
  ballistic missiles at the Saudi capital for a third straight night;
  Saudi-led coalition air defences intercept and destroy all three.
  Two days earlier a Houthi strike damaged the PIF Tower.
- **COP31 (today, 21 Nov):** climate talks in Türkiye drag into
  overtime as the fight over fossil-fuel wording deadlocks the deal.
- **Trump / Iran (20 Nov press conference, in 21 Nov briefings):**
  Trump threatens Iran with war over a rumoured "dirty bomb" plot on
  Washington; no plot has been confirmed publicly.
- **Denmark (announced 20 Nov, follow-up 21 Nov):** Denmark expels its
  Russian defence attaché over an alleged plot to kill a US-Ukrainian
  on Danish soil, and halts non-essential visas for Russians.
- **Markets (20 Nov close, in 21 Nov briefings):** Nasdaq wipes out
  all its 2026 gains as the AI sell-off sinks Anthropic's IPO bounce;
  Nasdaq 100 down 5.61% on Friday.
- **FBI (today, 21 Nov):** FBI investigating a suspected hack of its
  national crime database, the NCIC.
- **Qatar / Germany (20 Nov Berlin visit, in 21 Nov briefings):**
  Qatar pledges €15bn more investment in Germany in a "360-degree
  partnership", with two security and defence agreements signed.
- **Also today:** Basra gas pipeline blast — several killed, 30+
  injured, toll disputed between outlets (not stated as a flat
  number); Iran *claims* a 58th US MQ-9 drone downed (claim only, no
  US confirmation); Europe's youth protests roll into Saturday; a
  magnitude 6.7 quake shakes Peru's Andes; a pancreatic cancer pill
  (daraxonrasib) shrank tumours in 60.7% of trial patients; UEFA fines
  England €15,000 over discriminatory chants.

Sources (each story verified against 2+ outlets): Al Jazeera, Reuters,
Arab News, Saudi Press Agency, BBC, AP, AFP, The Guardian, Anadolu
Agency, WION, CNN, NBC News, Times of India, India Today, Euronews,
Shafaq News, The National, Iran International, Tasnim, RTÉ, Sky
Sports — 20–21 November 2026 editions. Full source list with URLs in

## Files

- `entry.png` — the entry image (1024x1024, < 1 MiB).

  Run `python3 draw.py` to regenerate it.
